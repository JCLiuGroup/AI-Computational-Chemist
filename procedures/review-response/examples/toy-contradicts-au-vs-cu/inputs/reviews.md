## Reviewer 1

**R1.C1** (p.3) — "The claim that gold's nobility confers greater resistance to
vacancy formation than copper is a chemical intuition presented without
evidence. A straightforward first-principles estimate of the monovacancy
formation energy in bulk Au versus Cu would either substantiate this central
argument or expose it as a non sequitur."

**R1.C2** (p.6) — "Throughout, 'noble' is used as if it implied mechanical or
thermodynamic robustness; the authors should define the term where first used."
